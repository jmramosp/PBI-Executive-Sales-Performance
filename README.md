## Project Overview

Executive Sales Performance is a Business Intelligence solution developed in Microsoft Power BI to provide a consolidated view of commercial performance, revenue, profitability, and sales operations.

The dashboard integrates approximately 60,000 transactional records with branch-level information across Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, and Mexico, enabling multidimensional analysis of sales activities.

The solution provides business stakeholders with interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs) to evaluate commercial results, monitor profitability, and identify opportunities for improvement.

## Business Objectives

The dashboard focuses on five key analytical areas:

- **Sales Performance:** Monitor sales revenue, transaction volumes, and sales trends.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Evaluate gross margins and associated product costs.
- **Branch Performance:** Compare commercial results across different locations.
- **Product & Discount Analysis:** Identify products with the highest discounts and analyze their commercial impact.
- **Sales Team Performance:** Evaluate sales performance across individual representatives.

## Data Overview

The Power BI data model includes two primary tables:

### Sales Transactions

Approximately 60,000 records containing:

- Transaction dates and identifiers
- Product names, categories, and suppliers
- Quantities sold
- Unit costs and sales prices
- Discounts and net sales values
- Sales representatives
- Branch identifiers

### Subsidiaries

Branch-level reference information containing:

- Branch identifiers and names
- Cities and countries
- Geographic coordinates

The dataset covers commercial operations across four Latin American countries.

## Technical Implementation

**Data Preparation — Power Query**

Data preparation and transformation to support consistent reporting and analytical calculations.

**Data Modeling — Power BI**

Organization of transactional and branch-level data to support analysis across locations, products, and sales representatives.

**Business Metrics — DAX**

Development of analytical measures covering revenue, sales quantities, gross margins, and discounts.

**Data Visualization — Power BI**

Development of an interactive executive dashboard featuring KPI cards, trend charts, branch comparisons, product analysis, and dynamic filters.

## Dashboard Capabilities

- Revenue and sales volume comparison by branch
- Top-performing sales representatives
